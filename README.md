# My Trainer

Personal, single-user training and nutrition app. No accounts or login: it opens straight to the dashboard.

- **Settings/Profile**: edit anytime; workouts and nutrition recalculate instantly.
- **Workouts**: split chosen from training days/week, exercises filtered by your equipment, sets/reps by goal and experience, with logging.
- **Nutrition (OMAD)**: calories and macros (Mifflin-St Jeor), plus a one-meal builder that respects dislikes, allergies and restrictions.
- **Progress**: weight log and trend.

Data lives in browser localStorage behind a small `store` adapter in `index.html`; replace it with an API/DB later. Open `index.html` to use.
